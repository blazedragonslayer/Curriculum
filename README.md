

## **User Profile:**

As the user profile, I used my information to use the exercise to serve as a first curriculum, but at the end of the 3-year to put the projects I am currently working in the curriculum.  Because of this, the user profile is: A 3rd-year software engineering student who is studying at the UdG, the University of Girona, and is interested in working in the summer in a software or computer company, as they want to learn more about coding or software in general or how today's computers work at a more in-depth level.

**Information architecture:**  
After the title, I put the most important information first: the summary of the curriculum, so they have a better idea of me, and the contact information if they want to contact me for an interview. After that, I put the experience that I have in a work or volunteer environment or the experience that I have in Software engineering. I put the volunteer experience first because it is the most important, as it is more similar to a work environment, and I have done it for 2 years, each year in a different role. The academic project, while it is more relevant to applying to a software engineering company they are more simple that i have descrived and I don’t think they are more important alse at the moment they are not in the Github page in the contact section. There is not much content because there isn’t much I can add that is relevant and is not just to fill space, and I don’t want to bloat the curriculum with useless information.

The summary and the experience, I put them on the right and in the biggest block, as they are more important. The left section is to complement the right with the university I say I’m going to and all the relevant courses I attended at the university. In the last part of the left section, there are the hard skills relevant to the curriculum.

**Visual design:**  
As it is a curriculum, there shouldn’t be that many cosmetic parts, as that is not appropriate, so I went for a more minimalistic look, focusing on the information rather than the look of the page. I didn’t change the font because it is already a good font for such a document, and I also guarantee that the text can always be shown. I have the entire left section with a background colour to differentiate the types of information more clearly and to highlight the easy-to-read important information. To highlight different sections of information, the headers are in bold and are accompanied by a horizontal bar.

To more easily understand how much I can speak the language, there is a progress bar at the level I can speak the specific language.

The colour palette is also very simple, only using 2 colours that I specifically chose because there are not very flashy colours while also being vibrant enough so that the page doesn’t feel moody. I used the colour blue for the title because it is a commonly used colour in professional documents, and I used the colour green to highlight the left section, as it combines well with the blue and is somewhat similar to blue.
